Alpha Score Quantitative Methodology

The Alpha Score (0.0 to 10.0) is an institutional cross-sectional metric that standardizes and combines short-term return velocity, medium-term momentum persistence, and trading liquidity.

$$\text{Alpha Score} = \text{Scale}_{0-10}\left( w_{24} \cdot Z_{24h} + w_{7d} \cdot Z_{7d} + w_{\text{vol}} \cdot Z_{\ln(\text{vol})} \right)$$
